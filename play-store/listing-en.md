**App name** (≤30): `Barajyq — Events & Places`

**Short description** (≤80):


**Full description** (≤4000):
```
Barajyq is an events guide for your city in Kazakhstan. Pick your city and interests, and get a feed of events and places that match them.

What Barajyq does:
• An events feed based on your interests: concerts, theatre, exhibitions and museums, cinema, stand-up, sports, runs, IT meetups, festivals, kids' events and free events.
• Places: museums, galleries, parks and nature spots, with opening hours and prices.
• One-tap directions: opens 2GIS with the venue address.
• The Tickets button opens the event's official page.
• "Not interested" hides what you don't like; you can bring it back in Settings.
• Cities across Kazakhstan: Astana, Almaty, Shymkent, Karaganda, Aktobe, Atyrau and more.
• Three languages: Kazakh, Russian, English.
• Sign in with Google; your settings stay on your device.

Barajyq shows no ads and does not sell your data. Your interests, city and language are stored only on your device.

Each card shows where the event came from. Spotted a schedule mistake? Let us know.
```
